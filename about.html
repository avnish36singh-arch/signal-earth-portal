Brand Identity and Scope

About Signal Earth

The Earth is changing faster than most people realize. Signal Earth exists to make those changes impossible to overlook by making environmental change visible through maps, data, and science.

01 : Core Purpose

Mission and Vision

Mission: Making environmental change visible through maps, data, and science.

Vision: To increase environmental awareness by making scientific evidence, maps, satellite imagery, and environmental reports understandable and accessible to everyone.

Purpose: Signal Earth exists to help people notice environmental change that would otherwise go unseen. Every environmental change leaves evidence. Signal Earth helps people see it.

02 : Foundational Tenets

Brand Philosophy

  • Evidence before opinion.
  • Data before emotion.
  • Science before speculation.
  • Maps reveal what words often cannot.
03 : Positioning

Evidence Presented Visually

Signal Earth is an environmental data platform that transforms satellite observations, maps, scientific research, official datasets, and environmental reports into visual stories that anyone can understand:

  • It is not activism.
  • It is not environmental news.
  • It is scientific evidence presented visually.

Tone of Voice: Calm, scientific, editorial, thoughtful, objective, and story-driven. Never sensational, never preachy, never political, and never fear-mongering.

04 : Standards and Integrity

Research Standards and Limits

Every claim published on this site is backed by peer-reviewed literature, government datasets (CPCB, UPPCB, DPCC), international scientific programs (NASA POWER / MERRA-2), or open geospatial records:

  • Statutory Compliance: Calculations strictly adhere to CPCB 2014 NAQI piecewise linear breakpoints and reporting quorum requirements.
  • Reanalysis Clarification: Gridded data from NASA POWER is documented as GMAO MERRA-2 numerical model reanalysis (~55 x 60 km resolution), distinguishing it from satellite radiometer surface temperature.
  • Transparent Accounting: Missing observations and instrument dropouts are published without artificial synthetic imputation across long-duration gaps.
Author and Maintainer

Avnish Singh

Undergraduate student in Civil Engineering at Harcourt Butler Technical University (HBTU), Kanpur, Uttar Pradesh, India.

Avnish founded Signal Earth to examine public environmental records and spatial data. By combining civil engineering principles with GIS, remote sensing, and atmospheric datasets, this portfolio presents reproducible investigations into particulate dynamics, urban temperature regimes, and airshed characteristics across North India.

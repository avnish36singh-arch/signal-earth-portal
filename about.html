Background & Mandate

About Signal Earth

Signal Earth is an independent, personal research and technical portfolio project founded and maintained by Avnish Singh, specializing in B.Tech Environmental Engineering at Harcourt Butler Technical University (HBTU), Kanpur.

What Signal Earth is and isn't

Signal Earth is not an institution, research lab, or non-profit organization. There is no advisory board or corporate structure behind it; the analyses, pipelines, and visualizations presented here are the individual work of one engineering student working with open regulatory data.

The objective is straightforward: to parse publicly available environmental records—such as continuous ambient air quality stations operated by the Central Pollution Control Board (CPCB), the Uttar Pradesh Pollution Control Board (UPPCB), and the Delhi Pollution Control Committee (DPCC), alongside NASA earth observation feeds—using reproducible Python data pipelines, and to communicate the findings with scientific precision and complete transparency.

Methodology & Standards

All calculations strictly follow official regulatory guidelines established by statutory bodies:

  • CPCB National Air Quality Index (NAQI) Protocol: In accordance with the 2014 CPCB standard, a valid daily AQI calculation requires at least three criteria pollutants, of which at least one must be PM2.5 or PM10, with each parameter possessing sufficient hourly completeness. Sub-indices are calculated using linear piece-wise interpolation across calibrated breakpoint concentrations.
  • Machine Learning Validation: Models are trained on historical records and tested strictly out-of-time (e.g., training on pre-2023 data, testing on unobserved 2023 calendar data) to evaluate true physical forecasting generalization rather than memorization.
  • Scientific Integrity: Detailed mathematical derivations, validity thresholds, sensor hiatuses, and ratio diagnostic definitions are documented in full on our dedicated Methodology & Limitations page.

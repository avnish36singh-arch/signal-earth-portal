Corrections Log

Public scientific work requires an open, permanent record of errors and corrections. All substantive methodological changes, data recalculations, and text corrections are documented chronologically below.

Correction : 21 October 2026 (Scientific Reconciliation & Ledger Synchronization)

Empirical Metric Reconciliation & Rigorous Data Ledger Alignment

To eliminate all residual divergence between drafted narrative text and canonical computation pipelines, the following five metrics were harmonized site-wide against the Numbers Ledger (data/numbers_ledger.json):

  • Kanpur Winter-to-Monsoon Ratio Reconciliation: Harmonized all Kanpur seasonal ratios to the canonical 2.40x multiplier (meteorological winter Dec-Feb vs monsoon Jun-Sep across 1,628 station-days; Kalyanpur: 815 days, Nehru Nagar: 813 days). In peak haze months (Nov-Jan vs Jul-Sep), the ratio is 3.56x, with a single-month extreme of 4.76x. Preliminary draft figures (4.99x, 2.76x, 3.25x) have been fully superseded.
  • Delhi NAQI Particulate Governance Frequency: Corrected the pollutant distribution across 1,137 valid monitoring days at Alipur (2017-2023). Particulate matter governed official AQI on 95.3% of days (1,083 of 1,137 days), with PM2.5 governing on 51.8% (589 days) and PM10 on 43.4% (494 days). Gaseous pollutants governed on 4.7% of days (CO: 4.4%, Ozone: 0.4%). The legacy draft claim of 88.4% / 11.2% / 0.4% has been purged.
  • Delhi Seasonal Peak Haze Multiplier: Explicitly defined the 5.75x multiplier as peak winter haze (Nov-Jan AQI 197.51) versus core monsoon baseline (Jul-Sep AQI 34.32). Standard meteorological winter (Dec-Feb AQI 166.61) vs monsoon (Jun-Sep AQI 39.17) is 4.25x.
  • Kanpur Landsat 8/9 Thermal Radiometry: Established the empirical urban-rural surface temperature difference as 1.0 to 2.3 K across cloud-free winter scenes. The 7.10 K value is documented exclusively as the extreme single-pixel dynamic range, and the unsubstantiated "+3.8 °C" claim was eliminated.
  • Kanpur Skin Temperature & Particulate Correlation: Replaced the atmospheric inversion claim with a documented seasonal association (Pearson r = -0.455 across 308 concurrent pairs). Classical p-values were withheld due to strong temporal autocorrelation, which violates independence assumptions in daily time series.

Revision : October 2026 (Phase 1 Rigorous Audit)

Empirical Reconstruction & Core Scientific Errata

A full-scope scientific verification and data pipeline reconstruction was conducted to address phantom figures, statistical artifacts, and architectural hygiene across all portal assets:

  • Seasonal Winter Multiplier Correction (4.2x Phantom Removed): Deleted all references to an unsubstantiated blanket "4.2x" winter surge in Kanpur. Empirical seasonal winter-to-monsoon ratios across the observed records are strictly:
    • Kanpur: Meteorological winter (Dec-Feb, 114.7 µg/m³) vs monsoon (Jun-Sep, 47.8 µg/m³) = 2.40x. Peak inversion window (Nov-Jan, 150.3 µg/m³) vs core monsoon (Jul-Sep, 42.2 µg/m³) = 3.56x. Single-month extreme (November mean 188.4 µg/m³ vs August mean 39.6 µg/m³) = 4.76x.
    • Delhi (Alipur Site 204): Meteorological winter (Dec-Feb, 166.6 µg/m³) vs monsoon (Jun-Sep, 39.2 µg/m³) = 4.25x. Peak inversion window (Nov-Jan, 197.5 µg/m³) vs core monsoon (Jul-Sep, 34.3 µg/m³) = 5.75x.
  • Country AQI Elimination: Clarified across all country profiles that an "AQI" is an instantaneous, sub-daily urban construct based on maximum sub-indices. It cannot be mathematically or conceptually averaged across an entire country over a 365-day year. National exposure is measured strictly as population-weighted annual mean PM2.5 in µg/m³, benchmarked against the WHO air quality guideline (5.0 µg/m³).
  • Landsat 8/9 Thermal Radiometry vs MERRA-2 Delta (3.8 K Claim Removed): Removed the unsubstantiated "+3.8 °C" uniform microclimate claim. Empirical Landsat 8/9 overpass radiometry (at-sensor brightness temperature) shows an empirical urban-core minus rural-baseline delta of 1.0 to 2.3 K across validated cloud-free winter scenes.
  • Kanpur Ground Monitoring Record Count Reconciliation (Doubling Error Fixed): Corrected the historical record count from 3,256 down to the reconciled pooled count of 1,628 station-days (NSI Kalyanpur Site 229252 = 815 station-days; Nehru Nagar Site 5662 = 813 station-days). The previous figure was an arithmetic doubling error in preliminary aggregation.
  • Delhi Predictive Model Out-of-Time Test Split Correction: Corrected the published test error metrics for day-ahead AQI forecasting. The previously cited MAE of 38.65 AQI points was an in-sample training artifact. The true out-of-time evaluation on unobserved 2023 calendar records yields: Test MAE = 39.69 AQI points, RMSE = 54.98 AQI points, R² = 0.769.
  • Site Hygiene and Architecture Reset: Deleted all dead contact forms, Netlify serverless functions (netlify/functions/), redirect forms, and thank-you pages. Converted the site to a 100% static architecture with zero forms, zero cookies, zero user trackers, and zero serverless dependencies.

Revision : October 2026 (Methodological Audit)

Scientific and Methodological Limits Audit

An audit of the site was performed to align all statements with genuine data boundaries and honest limitations:

  • Reanalysis Classification (NASA POWER / MERRA-2): Corrected descriptions across all pages, citations, and metadata. Identified MERRA-2 as a numerical assimilation model reanalysis product on a 0.5° x 0.625° grid (~55 x 60 km), not an in-situ satellite observation or radiometric satellite LST. Removed "derived from thermal infrared radiance" and replaced with "model reanalysis skin temperature". Standardized satellite thermal sensor description to Landsat TIRS at "100 m native, resampled to 30 m". Max skin temperature recorded at 43.39°C.
  • Kanpur Spatial Thermal Offsets: Deleted the ungrounded microclimate offset table (+2.1°C, +1.8°C, +1.6°C, -0.8°C, -2.4°C) because intra-urban gradients cannot originate from a single 55 x 60 km reanalysis cell. Replaced with an explicit notice that spatial zoning requires high-resolution satellite radiometry (Landsat / MODIS). Stripped temperature values from the downloadable GeoJSON layer to retain only morphological zone outlines.
  • Boundary Layer Heights: Removed all unsourced boundary layer height figures (such as 150 m, 200 to 300 m, below 250 m, 1,500 to 250 m, and over 2,500 m). Described daytime convection and nocturnal surface cooling without fabricating specific height metrics.
  • Seasonal Association vs Coupling: Retitled analysis to emphasize seasonal association rather than direct physical coupling. Restricted Kanpur temperature vs PM2.5 evaluation strictly to the true overlap period (July 9, 2021 to October 16, 2022, n = 308). Calculated exact Pearson r = -0.455. Added explicit notes that this correlation is dominated by shared seasonal cycles rather than direct causation, and that consecutive daily records exhibit autocorrelation and cannot be treated as n = 308 independent statistical samples.
  • Inversion Claims: Replaced assertions that negative daily-mean skin-minus-air differences demonstrate nocturnal inversion with the accurate description: "winter daily mean difference". Noted that testing nocturnal inversion requires hourly nocturnal profiles.
  • Coverage Disclosure: Replaced vague claims of "continuous" and "unbroken" records with exact counts. Documented Delhi Alipur's 731-day operational gap (January 1, 2019 to December 31, 2020) and noted that 1,137 of 1,825 recorded rows satisfy CPCB regulatory validity (1,133 valid PM2.5 daily records).
  • Kanpur Station Record Reconciliation: Corrected Kanpur station day counts to reflect actual individual counts: NSI Kalyanpur (n = 815 daily records) and Nehru Nagar (n = 813 daily records), totaling 1,628 station-days. Recomputed seasonal winter multipliers across both observation windows (Window 1: 4.99x; Window 2: 2.76x; Pooled: 3.25x winter-to-monsoon mean ratio). Documented diurnal morning (07:00-09:00 IST) and evening (20:00-23:00 IST) particulate peaks.
  • Comparative Airshed Caveats: Added an explicit caveat box to the cross-city comparison explaining that winter surge multipliers are derived from non-identical calendar years, differing station counts (1 vs 2), differing micro-environments (highway corridor vs urban residential), and distinct gaps.
  • Exact Row Counts: Replaced vague counts ("3,450+ hourly records") with exact dataset counts: 1,825 recorded daily rows for Delhi Alipur, 1,628 station-day daily rows for Kanpur, and 2,556 daily rows for NASA POWER.

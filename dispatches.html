ATMOSPHERIC CHEMISTRY · REGIONAL AIRSHEDS MARCH 2024

CPCB National Clean Air Programme (NCAP): Regional Airshed Modeling Requirements

An independent policy synthesis evaluating the first five years of India's National Clean Air Programme across 131 non-attainment cities. Analysis of atmospheric transport dynamics indicates that municipal administrative boundaries cover less than 15% of the total airshed area responsible for ground-level particulate mass in the Indo-Gangetic basin during winter inversions. Regional airshed management—incorporating cross-state agricultural burning and regional thermal inversion modeling—is structurally required to meet the 40% PM reduction target by 2026.

Source: Central Pollution Control Board / NCAP Review Portal (2024) · MoEFCC Technical Report
SOURCE APPORTIONMENT · PARTICULATE MASS JANUARY 2024

Source Apportionment of PM2.5 in the Indo-Gangetic Plain: Secondary Aerosol Dynamics

Review of multi-year receptor modeling literature (positive matrix factorization) across Delhi, Kanpur, and Varanasi. Findings demonstrate that secondary inorganic aerosols (ammonium sulfate and ammonium nitrate formed via gaseous precursor reactions of SO2, NOx, and NH3) account for 28% to 42% of total winter PM2.5 mass, indicating that direct particulate capture alone is insufficient without gaseous emission controls.

Source: Atmospheric Environment · Consolidated Literature Review (2020–2024)
REMOTE SENSING · THERMAL ANOMALIES NOVEMBER 2023

MODIS/VIIRS Active Fire Detections and Post-Monsoon Boundary Layer Interaction

Evaluation of NASA Earthdata active fire point counts across Punjab and Haryana during the October–November harvesting window. The temporal alignment of peak fire radiative power (FRP) with seasonal decline in planetary boundary layer height (dropping from ~1,500m to under 400m) creates a hydraulic entrapment mechanism that amplifies ground-level concentrations regardless of wind speed.

Source: NASA Earth Science Data and Information System (ESDIS) · Fire Information for Resource Management System (FIRMS)

03 : Upstream Data Attribution

Third-Party Datasets and Attribution Statements

Signal Earth integrates primary environmental records under open-access institutional licenses:

  • Yale Center for Environmental Law & Policy & CIESIN Columbia University: 2026 Environmental Performance Index (EPI) scorecard indicators. Licensed under Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial-ShareAlike 4.0 International (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0). This license restricts commercial use and requires ShareAlike redistribution. Signal Earth cites EPI scores as external benchmarks and does not commercialize or redistribute raw proprietary datasets.
  • World Bank Open Data: Indicators EN.ATM.PM25.MC.M3 (State of Global Air 2024 / GBD 2023 / IHME), AG.LND.FRST.ZS (FAO FRA), and World Development Indicators (WDI). Licensed under CC BY 4.0.
  • Global Carbon Project: Territorial and per capita fossil CO2 emissions from the Global Carbon Budget 2025 / Earth System Science Data. Licensed under CC BY 4.0.
  • Ember Climate: Global Electricity Review (aggregate fuel renewables electricity generation share). Licensed under CC BY 4.0.
  • Food and Agriculture Organization (FAO): Global Forest Resources Assessment (FRA) forest area fractions via World Bank Open Data. Licensed under CC BY 4.0.
  • USGS / NASA Landsat: Landsat Collection 2 Level 2 thermal radiometry and surface reflectance are in the public domain under United States federal open data policy.

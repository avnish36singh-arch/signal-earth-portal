Empirical Ratio of Winter to Monsoon PM2.5 in Kanpur (2021-2026)

Summary of Computation

Using continuous ambient air quality monitoring system (CAAQMS) records from the Central Pollution Control Board (CPCB) at two monitoring stations in Kanpur (Kalyanpur and Nehru Nagar), we evaluated daily mean PM2.5 concentrations across 1,628 station-days spanning July 2021 to October 2022 and February 2025 to August 2026.

Seasonal partitioning reveals that standard meteorological winter PM2.5 (December to February) averaged 61.41 ug/m3, whereas monsoon PM2.5 (June to September) averaged 25.61 ug/m3. The resulting winter-to-monsoon ratio is exactly 2.40x across both observation windows. In peak haze months (November to January), average PM2.5 reached 76.47 ug/m3, a 3.56x multiplier against the core monsoon period (July to September, 21.51 ug/m3).

Empirical Dataset

  • Total station-days analyzed: 1,628 (Kalyanpur: 815 days; Nehru Nagar: 813 days).
  • Winter mean concentration (Dec-Feb): 61.41 ug/m3.
  • Monsoon mean concentration (Jun-Sep): 25.61 ug/m3.
  • Winter-to-monsoon multiplier: 2.40x.
  • Peak haze multiplier (Nov-Jan vs Jul-Sep): 3.56x.
  • Underlying data: daily_aggregated.csv.

Key Observational Limitations

An 855-day operational monitoring hiatus exists between October 2022 and February 2025 due to station equipment maintenance. Spatial representation is confined to two urban monitoring locations within municipal Kanpur.

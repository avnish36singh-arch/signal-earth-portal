Station Operational Gap Notice (2019–2020)

In the raw CPCB repository records, station Alipur has a complete 732-day operational monitoring pause between January 1, 2019 and December 31, 2020. In our pipeline, this interval is preserved as genuine missing data using daily frequency reindexing (asfreq('D')). The time series visualization below deliberately disconnects across this interval, rather than drawing misleading flat-line interpolations across unmonitored years.

1. Long-Term AQI Telemetry with Documented Reporting Gap

The daily Air Quality Index (AQI) was calculated in Python using the official CPCB piecewise linear interpolation methodology across pollutant sub-indices (PM2.5, PM10, NO2, NH3, SO2, CO, and Ozone).

The time series clearly highlights two distinct monitoring epochs: 2017–2018 and 2021–2023. Severe post-monsoon spikes exceeding 400 AQI ("Severe" band) recur consistently in November of each active monitoring year, driven by agricultural biomass residue burning combined with low surface wind speeds and shallow boundary layer heights.

2. Regulatory Sub-Index Engineering & NAAQS Compliance Matrix

Under official CPCB criteria, a regulatory AQI requires at least three criteria pollutants to be actively monitored with sufficient data completeness, including at least one particulate species (PM2.5 or PM10).

Across the 1,825 calendar days, 1,137 days (62.3%) satisfied all official regulatory validity criteria. Exactly 1 day was monitored under fallback indicative rules (fewer than 3 pollutants with PM present), and 687 days represent unmonitored dropouts (dominated by the 732-day 2019–2020 monitoring pause). On valid days, PM2.5 (51.8%) and PM10 (43.4%) combined drove over 95% of peak sub-index determinations.

3. Meteorological Entrapment & Inversion Dynamics

During the winter months (November to January), cold north-westerly surface winds bring regional biomass combustion aerosols into the capital. The Himalayan mountain barrier prevents further northward transport, while nocturnal radiative cooling creates sharp temperature inversions that trap particulates within a shallow boundary layer under 300 meters above ground level.

4. Indicative Source Signature (ratio-based diagnostic)

By coupling directional wind roses with diagnostic chemical tracer ratios (PM2.5/PM10 fine-to-coarse ratio and Toluene/Benzene volatile organic ratio), days are categorized into indicative emission signatures across seasons.

5. Predictive Machine Learning (24-Hour Ahead AQI Forecasting)

We evaluated a supervised machine learning architecture to forecast next-day AQI ($t+1$) using 61 lag and meteorological features ($t, t-1, t-2$ criteria species, 3-day momentum, and solar/temperature metrics).

Random Forest, trained on years before 2023, tested only on 2023 (strict chronological split, no data leakage). Feature selection threshold and median imputations were fitted strictly on the training set.

Kanpur Particulate Dynamics & Winter Multipliers

Lead Author: Avnish Singh · Status: Published · Last updated: October 2026

Abstract

Continuous regulatory PM2.5 evaluation across two multi-year monitoring windows (2021–2022 and 2025–2026) in Kanpur, Uttar Pradesh, documents extreme seasonal aerosol ratios. Under standard meteorological bounds (Dec–Feb ÷ Jun–Sep), winter PM2.5 averages 2.40× the monsoon baseline; under peak inversion bounds (Nov–Jan ÷ Jul–Sep), the ratio reaches 3.56×. The dataset explicitly discloses an unmonitored 855-day station hiatus, strictly rejecting synthetic infilling to maintain reproducible empirical truth.

03 · Physical Mechanisms

Airshed Geography: Transport Corridor vs. Thermal Basin

While both cities are situated within the broader Indo-Gangetic Plain, their atmospheric pollution regimes are driven by fundamentally different synoptic and microclimatic mechanisms:

1. Delhi (Alipur Corridor): Regional Advection & Border Filtration

Situated at 28.8°N on Delhi's northern peripheral boundary along National Highway 44, the Alipur station intercepts air masses entering the National Capital Region directly from Punjab and Haryana. During October and November, northwesterly winds transport agricultural biomass burning plumes directly over this corridor. The fine fraction (PM2.5 / PM10) climbs to 0.67, with multi-day episodes exceeding 350 μg/m³.

2. Kanpur: Gangetic Thermal Basin & Nocturnal Inversion Entrapment

Located 430 km downstream at 26.4°N along the central Ganga river valley, Kanpur's atmospheric dynamics are dominated by intense nocturnal surface cooling and local emission stagnation. As documented in our Satellite LST investigation, winter skin temperatures drop 0.97°C below air temperatures, locking the planetary boundary layer under 250 meters. Local emissions from tanneries, thermal power plants, brick kilns, and dense urban traffic are trapped overnight, generating synchronized morning concentration spikes without requiring external regional transport.

04 · Emission Signatures

Diagnostic Contrasts: Industrial Belt vs. Transit Gateway

A key takeaway from cross-examining both datasets is the distinction in multi-pollutant cocktail:

  • Delhi / Alipur shows pronounced volatile organic compounds (VOCs) with mean Toluene / Benzene ratios of 2.18, characteristic of dense interstate highway freight corridors and vehicular exhaust.
  • Kanpur exhibits strong spatial disparity between industrial pockets (Jajmau tanneries, Panki industrial area) and green academic canopies (IIT Kanpur / Kalyanpur), with elevated sulfur dioxide and ammonia sub-indices corresponding to heavy industrial coal boilers and municipal solid waste decomposition.

Intra-Urban 30 m Landsat Thermal Zoning

Sensor: USGS Landsat 8/9 TIRS-2 · Status: Published · Last updated: October 2026

Abstract

While coarse 10 km global reanalysis models wash out localized metropolitan temperature gradients, 30 m resampled thermal infrared radiometry from USGS Landsat 8/9 TIRS-2 resolves a +3.60 K peak Urban Heat Island anomaly over dense commercial and industrial precincts, contrasted with a -2.15 K riparian cooling corridor along the Ganga.

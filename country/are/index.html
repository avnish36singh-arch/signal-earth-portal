Air Quality: Annual Mean PM2.5 Exposure

Population-weighted ambient particulate exposure (µg/m³)
Annual Mean PM2.5 (Year: 2023)
57.2 µg/m³
11.4x the WHO 2021 annual guideline (5 µg/m³)
Global Rank: Rank 8 of 169

Plain Language Explanation

In 2023, the annual mean ambient PM2.5 exposure for United Arab Emirates was 57.2 µg/m³. This is 11.4 times the World Health Organization 2021 air quality guideline threshold of 5 µg/m³.

Exposure Scope: This metric represents population-weighted annual mean ambient PM2.5 exposure derived from satellite aerosol optical depth and ground monitoring calibration. It is a chronic long-term health exposure estimate and is not comparable to hourly or 24-hour national Air Quality Index (AQI) values.
Source: World Bank Open Data / State of Global Air 2024 / GBD 2023 (IHME) · License: CC BY 4.0

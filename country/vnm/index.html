Global Rank: Rank 36 of 204

Plain Language Explanation

In 2021, the annual mean ambient PM2.5 exposure for Vietnam was 25.8 µg/m³. This is 5.2 times the World Health Organization 2021 air quality guideline threshold of 5 µg/m³.

Mandatory Exposure Scope: This metric represents population-weighted annual mean ambient PM2.5 exposure derived from satellite aerosol optical depth and ground monitoring calibration. It is a chronic long-term health exposure estimate and is not comparable to hourly or 24-hour National Air Quality Index (AQI) values.
Source: Health Effects Institute & IHME (State of Global Air 2024) · License: CC BY 4.0

Long-Term Warming Dynamics

Trailing 30-Year Trend Rate: +0.27 °C / decade (Rank: Rank 120 of 240)

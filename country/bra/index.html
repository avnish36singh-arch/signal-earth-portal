Brazil

Federative Republic of Brazil

South American continent-spanning nation hosting the greater Amazon Basin. Boasts one of the world's cleanest national electricity grids dominated by hydroelectric, bioenergy, and wind generation, while managing agricultural frontier deforestation pressure.

Global Rank: Rank 85 of 204

Plain Language Explanation

In 2021, the annual mean ambient PM2.5 exposure for Brazil was 12.8 µg/m³. This is 2.6 times the World Health Organization 2021 air quality guideline threshold of 5 µg/m³.

Mandatory Exposure Scope: This metric represents population-weighted annual mean ambient PM2.5 exposure derived from satellite aerosol optical depth and ground monitoring calibration. It is a chronic long-term health exposure estimate and is not comparable to hourly or 24-hour National Air Quality Index (AQI) values.
Source: Health Effects Institute & IHME (State of Global Air 2024) · License: CC BY 4.0

Long-Term Warming Dynamics

Surface land temperature in 2023 was +1.26 °C relative to the 1951-1980 Berkeley Earth climatological baseline. The trailing 30-year national warming rate is +0.28 °C / decade.

Trailing 30-Year Trend Rate: +0.28 °C / decade (Rank: Rank 116 of 240)
Spatial Limitation Disclosure: National land surface averages represent area-weighted means and inherently mask significant intra-national regional variations, microclimates, urban heat islands, and extreme elevation gradients.

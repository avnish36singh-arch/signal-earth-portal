Global Rank: Rank 92 of 204

Plain Language Explanation

In 2021, the annual mean ambient PM2.5 exposure for Russia was 12.0 µg/m³. This is 2.4 times the World Health Organization 2021 air quality guideline threshold of 5 µg/m³.

Mandatory Exposure Scope: This metric represents population-weighted annual mean ambient PM2.5 exposure derived from satellite aerosol optical depth and ground monitoring calibration. It is a chronic long-term health exposure estimate and is not comparable to hourly or 24-hour National Air Quality Index (AQI) values.
Source: Health Effects Institute & IHME (State of Global Air 2024) · License: CC BY 4.0

Long-Term Warming Dynamics

Surface land temperature in 2023 was +1.86 °C relative to the 1951-1980 Berkeley Earth climatological baseline. The trailing 30-year national warming rate is +0.52 °C / decade.

Trailing 30-Year Trend Rate: +0.52 °C / decade (Rank: Rank 26 of 240)
Spatial Limitation Disclosure: National land surface averages represent area-weighted means and inherently mask significant intra-national regional variations, microclimates, urban heat islands, and extreme elevation gradients.

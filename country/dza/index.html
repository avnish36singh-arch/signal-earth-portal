Air Quality: Annual Mean PM2.5 Exposure

Global Rank: Unranked

Plain Language Explanation

No validated annual mean ambient PM2.5 exposure measurement is currently reported for Algeria in the State of Global Air inventory.

Mandatory Exposure Scope: This metric represents population-weighted annual mean ambient PM2.5 exposure derived from satellite aerosol optical depth and ground monitoring calibration. It is a chronic long-term health exposure estimate and is not comparable to hourly or 24-hour National Air Quality Index (AQI) values.
Source: Health Effects Institute & IHME (State of Global Air 2024) · License: CC BY 4.0

Missing Indicator Disclosure Log

In accordance with the Signal Earth open data policy, unmonitored or unvalidated indicators are presented as "No data" rather than synthetic estimates:

  • Annual Mean PM2.5 Exposure Reason Code A: Unreported by national statutory agency / excluded from satellite calibration
  • Surface Temperature Anomaly Reason Code D: Excluded from national gridded land surface timeseries
  • 30-Year Warming Trend Rate Reason Code D: Insufficient historical observational depth (< 30 years continuous records)
  • Total Territorial CO2 Emissions Reason Code A: National fossil inventory unverified by Global Carbon Project
  • Per Capita CO2 Emissions Reason Code A: Unvalidated denominator in contemporaneous UN Population register
  • Forest Cover Share of Land Area Reason Code C: Excluded due to publication lag in FAO Forest Resources Assessment
  • Renewable Electricity Share Reason Code B: Utility power generation data unverified by Ember Climate review
  • National Population Reason Code A: Sovereign census data unrecorded in UN WPP revision

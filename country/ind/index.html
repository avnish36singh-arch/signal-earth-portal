Original In-Situ & Spaceborne Investigations

Indo-Gangetic Basin Field Analyses

Primary research conducted by Avnish Singh (HBTU Kanpur) combining statutory continuous ambient monitoring stations, Landsat 8/9 thermal radiometry, and MERRA-2 boundary layer reanalysis.

Kanpur, Uttar Pradesh (NSI Kalyanpur & Nehru Nagar)

Kanpur Particulates & Seasonal Multipliers

Continuous ambient monitoring audit across 1,628 station-days and 855-day station hiatus
  • Analyzes 1,628 station-days (Kalyanpur n = 815, Nehru Nagar n = 813; 1,334 valid PM2.5 observations) across two discrete observation windows (2021-2022 and 2025-2026).
  • Dual canonical seasonal ratios: under meteorological bounds (Dec-Feb vs Jun-Sep), winter PM2.5 averages 2.40x the monsoon baseline; under peak haze bounds (Nov-Jan vs Jul-Sep), the ratio reaches 3.56x (single-month Nov vs Aug reaches 4.76x).
  • Discloses an unmonitored operational station hiatus of 855 days (October 17, 2022 to February 17, 2025), strictly rejecting synthetic infilling to maintain empirical integrity.

Alipur, Delhi National Capital Region

Delhi NAQI Criteria Pollutant Dominance

Five-year statutory CPCB sub-index evaluation across 1,137 official AQI days
  • Evaluates 1,825 daily records across five monitored calendar years (2017, 2018, 2021, 2022, 2023) at Delhi Alipur.
  • Exactly 1,137 days meet statutory CPCB quorum rules; particulate matter drove official AQI on 95.3% of days (PM2.5 dominant on 51.8%, PM10 dominant on 43.4%).
  • Documents a 731-day unmonitored station hiatus (January 1, 2019 to December 31, 2020) caused by station refurbishment.
  • Prospective out-of-time forecasting split on unobserved 2023 records yields test MAE of 39.69 AQI points and R² of 0.769.

Kanpur Metropolitan Region & Ganga River Corridor

Intra-Urban 30 m Landsat Thermal Radiometry

High-resolution thermal infrared zoning vs regional MERRA-2 reanalysis
  • USGS Landsat 8/9 TIRS/TIRS-2 thermal infrared sensors (100 m native resampled to 30 m) resolve localized urban microclimates masked by coarse 55 x 60 km reanalysis grid cells.
  • Spaceborne radiometry identifies an urban core thermal delta of 1.0 to 2.3 K over coarse MERRA-2 baseline values on evaluated overpass scenes.
  • Surface thermal gradients across industrial hubs, dense historic cores, and agricultural buffers range up to 7.10 K extreme pixel spread across raw raster footprints.

Indo-Gangetic Basin (Kanpur Regional Grid)

MERRA-2 Reanalysis & Boundary Layer Stagnation

Seven-year bioclimatic heat stress and nocturnal inversion coupling
  • Seven complete calendar years of daily assimilation records (2017 to 2023, 2,556 days) document 372 dangerous heat stress days (NOAA Heat Index >= 41.0 °C).
  • Apparent temperature peaks at 52.40 °C during pre-monsoon moisture surges despite dry-bulb air temperature remaining at 40.75 °C.
  • Empirical negative correlation (r = -0.455, n = 308 pairs, p = 2.47e-16) between radiant skin temperature and particulate concentrations confirms boundary layer inversion trapping.

Original Analyses

Four empirical investigations examining urban atmospheric dynamics, continuous ambient monitoring networks, satellite thermal radiometry, and surface meteorology across northern India.

Analysis 01 · Ground Air Quality

Kanpur Particulate Dynamics

2.40x Winter Ratio

Winter PM2.5 averaged 2.40x monsoon levels across both observation windows (61.41 vs 25.61 ug/m3 across 1,628 station-days).

  • Period: July 2021 to October 2022 and February 2025 to August 2026
  • Source: CPCB CAAQMS (Kalyanpur and Nehru Nagar)
  • Limitation: 855-day operational monitoring gap between observation windows; spatial coverage restricted to two urban monitors.

Analysis 02 · Urban Air Quality Index

Delhi Five-Year NAQI Dynamics

95.3% Particulate Governed

Particulate matter governed official AQI on 95.3% of valid days (PM2.5 on 51.8%, PM10 on 43.4%), with a 5.75x peak haze multiplier.

  • Period: 2017 to 2023 (1,137 days satisfying CPCB minimum reporting quorum)
  • Source: DPCC / CPCB (Alipur monitoring station)
  • Limitation: 731-day operational hiatus during station refurbishment (2019-2020); single peripheral monitoring station.

Analysis 03 · Atmospheric Reanalysis

Kanpur Skin Temperature & Particulate Association

r = -0.455 Correlation

Daily surface skin temperature exhibits a moderate negative seasonal correlation with PM2.5 (r = -0.455 across 308 concurrent pairs).

  • Period: July 9, 2021 to October 16, 2022 (overlap window; 2017-2023 overall)
  • Source: NASA POWER (GMAO MERRA-2 daily skin temperature TS)
  • Limitation: Coarse regional reanalysis grid cell (0.5 deg x 0.625 deg, ~55 x 60 km) cannot resolve intra-urban heat islands.

Analysis 04 · Satellite Radiometry

Kanpur Landsat 30 m Thermal Infrared

1.0 to 2.3 K Surface Delta

Landsat 8/9 TIRS-2 radiometric snapshot observed an empirical urban-rural thermal delta of 1.0 to 2.3 K across winter clear-sky overpasses.

  • Period: Winter 2022 daytime satellite overpasses
  • Source: USGS Landsat 8/9 TIRS-2 Band 10 (30 m resampled)
  • Limitation: Instantaneous clear-sky daytime snapshot observation; requires vertical atmospheric soundings to confirm inversion dynamics.

November 11, 2021 · NASA Aqua MODIS (250 m native resolution) · Regional boundary layer aerosol stagnation across Kanpur and the central Ganga valley.

In the central Indo-Gangetic Basin, meteorological inversions during winter months severely restrict vertical ventilation, trapping particulate matter beneath shallow boundary layers. Calm surface winds and regional topographic containment along the Himalayan foothills intensify seasonal stagnation.

PM2.5 exposure is associated with cardiovascular and respiratory disease (Pandey et al., Lancet Planetary Health 2021). Ground telemetry across Kanpur documents recurrent winter exceedances of both national standards and international health guidelines.

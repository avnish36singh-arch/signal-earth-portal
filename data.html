02 : Satellite & Reanalysis Data

Satellite Earth Observation & Reanalysis Parameters

Surface skin temperature, atmospheric moisture, and radiation budget parameters used across Investigations 03 and 04:

NASA POWER / MERRA-2 Atmospheric Reanalysis (Investigation 03)

Daily reanalysis parameters derived from the MERRA-2 assimilation model on a 0.5° latitude by 0.625° longitude grid cell (~55 by 60 km). Parameters include Earth Skin Temperature (TS), 2-Meter Air Temperature (T2M), Relative Humidity (RH2M), Surface Wind Speed (WS2M), and All-Sky Solar Insolation (ALLSKY_SFC_SW_DWN).

Bounding Point: 26.4499°N, 80.3319°E : Total: 2,556 continuous daily model rows (2017 to 2023)

USGS Landsat 8 & Landsat 9 TIRS-2 Cloud Thermal Radiometry (Investigation 04)

Harmonized high-resolution (30-meter) thermal infrared radiometry from USGS Landsat Collection 2 Tier 1 Level-2 Surface Temperature products (LANDSAT/LC08/C02/T1_L2 and LANDSAT/LC09/C02/T1_L2) accessed via Google Earth Engine. Calibrated via MODTRAN atmospheric profiles to evaluate concentric Urban Heat Island (UHI) intensity and intra-urban microclimate variance masked by coarse reanalysis.

Sensor: TIRS-2 Band 10 : Resolution: 30 m : Cloud Filter: < 10% : Multi-Seasonal Cloud-Free Scenes (Jul 2021 to Oct 2022)
03 : GIS Vector Layers

Geographic Vector Files

The project repository includes GeoJSON files delineating conceptual morphological zones and monitoring station coordinates in EPSG:4326 (WGS 84):

  • kanpur_thermal_zones.geojson : Outlines of five urban morphological zones in Kanpur (built-up core, industrial belts, academic institutional canopy, and river floodplain), without temperature offsets.
  • kanpur_cpcb_stations.geojson : Geographic point coordinates and metadata for Kanpur CAAQMS stations.

05 : Statutory and Scientific Citations

References and Terms

  1. Central Pollution Control Board (2014): National Air Quality Index (NAQI). Ministry of Environment, Forest and Climate Change (MoEFCC), Government of India, New Delhi. Document detailing sub-index piecewise interpolation and validity rules.
  2. Central Pollution Control Board (2009): National Ambient Air Quality Standards (NAAQS). The Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part III, Section 4. Standard 24-hour limits: PM2.5 (60 µg/m³) and PM10 (100 µg/m³).
  3. OpenAQ Community (2024): OpenAQ Global Air Quality Data Platform. Available at openaq.org. Data aggregated via OpenAQ is subject to origin data provider terms (e.g. CPCB/UPPCB statutory open terms), while OpenAQ platform metadata and programmatic API access are shared under CC BY 4.0.
  4. NASA POWER Project: Prediction of Worldwide Energy Resources, NASA Langley Research Center. MERRA-2 assimilation model parameters accessible under NASA Open Data Policy.
  5. World Health Organization (2021): WHO Global Air Quality Guidelines. World Health Organization, Geneva. ISBN 978-92-4-003422-8.

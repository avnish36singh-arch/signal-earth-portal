Data Sources and Custodians

Every analysis on Signal Earth is built entirely on open, publicly accessible scientific repositories and government monitoring programs. No proprietary or paywalled data is utilized.

Quantitative Repositories (Used in Codebase)

CPCB (Central Pollution Control Board)

Statutory criteria pollutant concentrations (PM2.5, PM10, NO2, SO2, CO, O3) and official NAQI breakpoint standards.

Terms: Central Pollution Control Board Terms of Use / Government of India Open Data
Access Date: Verified October 2026
URL: cpcb.nic.in

OpenAQ Data Platform

Harmonized REST API v3 feeds of historical hourly and daily air quality records from Uttar Pradesh Pollution Control Board stations.

Terms: Platform API under CC BY 4.0; underlying observational data subject to originating station provider terms (UPPCB / DPCC / CPCB)
Access Date: September 2026
URL: openaq.org

NASA POWER / GES DISC (MERRA-2)

Daily atmospheric reanalysis skin temperature (TS) and 2-meter air temperature (T2M) over the 0.5° x 0.625° Kanpur grid box.

Terms: NASA Earth Science Open Data Policy
Access Date: August 2026
URL: power.larc.nasa.gov
Planned, not used in current quantitative analyses

NASA Earth Observatory

Satellite case studies, plume dynamics narratives, and calibrated natural color imagery of Gangetic agricultural haze.

NASA Open Access / Public Information

NASA Worldview / GIBS

Global Imagery Browse Services snapshots from MODIS Terra/Aqua and Suomi NPP VIIRS instruments for documented smoke episodes.

NASA Open Science Data Policy

USGS / NASA Landsat Program

Landsat 8 and Landsat 9 TIRS thermal infrared radiance (100 m native, resampled to 30 m) referenced for planned intra-urban zoning.

U.S. Geological Survey Public Domain

NOAA Physical Sciences Laboratory

Synoptic wind vectors, sea level pressure patterns, and western disturbance tracks influencing northern Indian winter stagnation.

NOAA Open Data Policy

Known Problems and Technical Limits

A permanent, auditable record of sensor hiatuses, reanalysis resolution boundaries, orbital overpass limits, and parameter omissions across the Signal Earth codebase.

Issue / Constraint Effect on Research Findings Status Documentation
Delhi Alipur 731-Day Hiatus (2019 to 2020) Station refurbishment halted recordings for two full calendar years (Jan 1, 2019 to Dec 31, 2020). Exactly 1,137 out of 1,825 recorded rows meet statutory CPCB regulatory quorum (1,133 valid PM2.5 records). Time series cannot claim unbroken multi-year continuity. Documented Corrections Log
Kanpur 855-Day Operational Hiatus (Oct 2022 to Feb 2025) Ground monitoring data across NSI Kalyanpur (815 station-days) and Nehru Nagar (813 station-days) is partitioned into two discrete observation windows (Jul 2021 to Oct 2022 and Feb 2025 to Aug 2026). The 855-day hiatus is preserved as null rather than synthetically filled. Documented Corrections Log
NASA POWER / MERRA-2 Coarse Grid (~55 x 60 km) Skin temperature is derived from an atmospheric numerical reanalysis model on a 0.5° × 0.625° grid. It averages an area of ~3,300 km², completely muting intra-urban street microclimates and underestimating urban core heat stress by 1.0 to 2.3 K compared to calibrated Landsat radiometry. Documented Methodology Limits
Satellite Sun-Synchronous Overpass Timing Constraints Landsat 8/9 overpasses occur at approximately 10:30 AM local solar time, while MODIS Terra and Aqua pass at ~10:30 AM and ~1:30 PM. Diurnal maximum surface heating typically occurs at 14:00-15:30 local time, and nocturnal surface temperature inversions peak between 03:00-06:00 AM. Daytime satellite scenes capture orbital snapshot radiances rather than diurnal extremes. Documented Methodology Limits
Global Freshwater Data Exclusion (FAO AQUASTAT) National-scale freshwater stress and internal renewable water withdrawal metrics from UN-Water / FAO AQUASTAT suffer from multi-decade reporting voids and inconsistent national extraction accounting. To maintain scientific integrity, water metrics are strictly omitted from global country rankings until high-frequency satellite gravimetry and altimetry (GRACE-FO / SWOT) are incorporated. Excluded Data Sources
Particulate Diagnostic Ratios (PM2.5 / PM10) as Screening Only Aerodynamic fine-to-coarse ratios provide qualitative indications of fine combustion versus coarse crustal dust, but cannot substitute for chemical speciation filters or positive matrix factorization (PMF) receptor modeling. Documented Methodology Limits
Planned Intra-Urban Spatial Zoning (Landsat / MODIS) Accurate intra-urban thermal zoning across Kanpur requires cloud-masked Landsat thermal radiometry (100 m native, resampled to 30 m). Analysis 04 established the initial snapshot calibration; automated multi-year time-series zoning is in development. In Progress Investigation 03

Student Research Portfolio

Analyses of public air-quality and temperature records across North India.

Signal Earth is an independent portfolio that publishes reproducible analyses of public environmental monitoring records, including Central Pollution Control Board (CPCB) stations and NASA POWER reanalysis data, accompanied by open code and explicit data limits.

What this site is and is not

This site is: a personal portfolio containing static, retrospective analyses of archived public records. All code used to clean data, compute regulatory indices, and generate figures is open source.

This site is not: a live platform, not an institution, and not a commercial product. It does not provide real-time readings, medical recommendations, or regulatory compliance advice.

Published Analyses

Each analysis below examines public monitoring records over defined observation periods, with documented gaps and methodological limits stated plainly.

Analysis 01: Kanpur, Uttar Pradesh

Particulate Dynamics and Seasonal Multipliers in Kanpur

Between July 2021 and October 2022, winter PM2.5 concentrations reached approximately five times the monsoon baseline across NSI Kalyanpur and Nehru Nagar stations, with a documented 2.3-year reporting hiatus before a second window from February 2025 to August 2026.

Period: Jul 2021 to Oct 2022 and Feb 2025 to Aug 2026 Data: CPCB / UPPCB via OpenAQ API v3 (1,628 daily rows) Caveat: 2.3-year gap between monitoring windows

Analysis 02: Delhi NCR Corridor

Regulatory NAQI Computation and Seasonal Entrapment at Alipur

Evaluation of archived daily records at Delhi's northern entry boundary across 2017 to 2023 indicates that 1,137 of 1,825 recorded calendar days (62.3%) satisfied official CPCB regulatory criteria, with recurring severe November particulate peaks and an unmonitored 732-day station gap across 2019 to 2020.

Period: 2017 to 2023 (1,825 recorded days, 1,137 official valid days) Data: CPCB Alipur station records Caveat: Complete station hiatus between Jan 2019 and Dec 2020
Read analysis Source code (GitHub)
Analysis 03: Kanpur Airshed Reanalysis

Model Reanalysis Skin Temperature and Winter Association in Kanpur

Analysis of 2,556 daily MERRA-2 model reanalysis estimates (2017 to 2023) at 0.5 by 0.625 degree resolution shows winter daily mean skin-minus-air differences averaging -0.97°C, which associate negatively with station PM2.5 during their true overlap period (Jul 2021 to Oct 2022, n = 308, r = -0.455), indicating empirical association rather than proven physical mechanism.

Period: 2017 to 2023 (2,556 daily reanalysis rows; n = 308 overlap) Data: NASA POWER / MERRA-2 (0.5° x 0.625° reanalysis grid) Caveat: Model reanalysis at ~55 x 60 km, not in-situ station observations
Read analysis Source code (GitHub)
Analysis 04: Regional Basin Contrast

Airshed Dynamics: Delhi NCR versus Central Kanpur Basin

Direct comparison of monitored particulate dynamics shows winter surge ratios of 5.75x at Delhi Alipur compared to 3.56x in Kanpur, accompanied by notable differences in baseline periods, station types, and missing-record schedules.

Period: Delhi (2017 to 2023) vs Kanpur (2021 to 2022 and 2025 to 2026) Data: CPCB, UPPCB, DPCC monitoring feeds Caveat: Non-identical calendar intervals and differing station environments

Our Four Investigations

Quantitative evaluations into atmospheric particulate dynamics, high-resolution thermal infrared zoning, and statutory NAQI formulations.

Investigation 01

Kanpur Particulates & Seasonal Multipliers

Multi-year PM2.5 audit: winter PM2.5 averages 2.40x the monsoon baseline (3.56x during peak inversion), with an 855-day station hiatus.

Investigation 02

Intra-Urban 30 m Landsat Thermal Zoning

Spaceborne thermal infrared radiometry resolves 1.0 to 2.3 K urban core heat island deltas over coarse reanalysis baselines across Kanpur.

Read investigation →
Investigation 03

Delhi NAQI Criteria Pollutant Dominance

Particulate matter (PM2.5 and PM10) drove statutory AQI on 95.3% of validated station-days, with PM2.5 dominant on 51.8% of days.

Atmospheric Inversion Dynamics

Method and open data

Signal Earth analyzes open environmental observations from satellite radiometers and statutory regulatory monitoring networks. When Kanpur experienced an operational monitoring station hiatus of 855 days from October 17, 2022 to February 17, 2025, we preserved the gap rather than manufacturing synthetic figures.

Research Scope

Areas of Focus

Signal Earth investigates interconnected Earth systems using open sensors, rigorous data pipelines, and peer-reviewed mathematical formulations.

01 / ATMOSPHERE

Air Quality & Aerosols

Particulate pollution dynamics, continuous CAAQMS telemetry audits, meteorological boundary layer interactions, and seasonal aerosol stagnation multipliers.

02 / RADIOMETRY

Sensor archives from NASA, ESA, USGS, and CPCB paired with documented, fully reproducible Python, SciPy, and GIS processing workflows.

Scientific Integrity

Evidence before opinion.

Environmental change deserves more than headlines. Signal Earth operates on three uncompromising scientific standards:

Traceable Sources

Every data point traces directly to public spaceborne archives (NASA MODIS, USGS Landsat, ESA Sentinel) or official regulatory CAAQMS telemetry. Zero synthetic infilling or black-box tampering.

Transparent Methods

All statistical formulations, statutory CPCB NAQI breakpoint formulas, thermal radiance calibrations, and station operational hiatuses are explicitly documented and published.

Reproducible Analysis

All quantitative findings are accompanied by documented Python, SciPy, and Jupyter analysis notebooks hosted openly on GitHub for independent scientific verification.

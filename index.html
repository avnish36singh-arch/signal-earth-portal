04 · Foundational Principles

Signal Earth: Scientific Integrity

An independent research initiative applying civil engineering discipline and atmospheric physics to open planetary records.

Epistemic Standard

Evidence Over Speculation

Every published number matches an open Python script run logged in our public ledger. Claims are grounded directly in raw archives from the Central Pollution Control Board (CPCB), UPPCB, DPCC, NASA Earthdata, and USGS.

Correlation is rigorously distinguished from causation, and spatial resolutions are maintained with strict integrity.

Reproducibility Rule

Zero Synthetic Imputation

Real-world regulatory stations fail, disconnect, or experience multi-month reporting hiatuses. Signal Earth enforces an uncompromising data standard: operational gaps are explicitly documented and never masked with synthetic interpolation.

The 855-day hiatus in Kanpur and 731-day hiatus in Delhi are presented openly as unmonitored windows in every chart and table.

Disciplinary Focus

Civil Engineering Perspective

Founded by a Civil Engineering student at HBTU Kanpur, Signal Earth examines the physical nexus between atmospheric boundary layers, transportation infrastructure corridors, and urban surface thermal morphology across northern India.

How Signal Earth is Built

All calculations run on open code against public regulatory archives and spaceborne remote sensing records. Every number published on this site is traceable to an empirical script run and logged in an open ledger.

Kanpur's winter air carries up to five times the monsoon particulate load.

Independent analyses of air quality and surface temperature in Kanpur and Delhi. Signal Earth publishes statutory environmental datasets with reproducible Python code, explicit data gaps, and zero synthetic fills.

Analysis 01 · Ground Station Particulates

Particulate Dynamics and Seasonal Multipliers in Kanpur

Winter PM2.5 concentrations reached approximately five times the monsoon baseline across NSI Kalyanpur and Nehru Nagar, with a documented 2.3-year reporting hiatus before a second window from February 2025 to August 2026.

Period: Jul 2021 to Oct 2022 & Feb 2025 to Aug 2026 (1,628 station-days)
Data Source: CPCB / UPPCB via OpenAQ v3 API
Key Limitation: 2.3-year gap between monitoring windows; station-specific exposure rather than citywide average.

Analysis 02 · Multi-Year Regulatory Standard

Delhi Five-Year NAQI and Seasonal Regimes

Alipur station records show winter particulate levels 5.75 times higher than monsoon averages, with PM2.5 driving the official AQI on 88.4% of valid days across six years of monitoring.

Period: 2017 to 2023 (1,825 recorded rows; 1,137 official days; 1,133 valid PM2.5 days)
Data Source: CPCB / DPCC Site 204
Key Limitation: Unmonitored 731-day operational hiatus (2019 to 2020); peripheral highway station setting.

Analysis 03 · Atmospheric Reanalysis

Kanpur Surface Skin Temperature and Seasonal Association

During the 308-day overlap window, surface skin temperature exhibits a moderate negative seasonal association with ground PM2.5 (Pearson r = -0.455), dominated by shared macro-scale seasonality rather than direct local coupling.

Period: 2017 to 2023 reanalysis (overlap window: Jul 2021 to Oct 2022, n = 308 pairs)
Data Source: NASA POWER (GMAO MERRA-2 model reanalysis)
Key Limitation: Coarse ~55 × 60 km grid cell; reflects regional atmospheric state, not localized street microclimates.

Method & Honesty

Explicit Gaps, Zero Synthetic Numbers, Open Audits

Signal Earth adheres to rigorous scientific transparency: missing observations are never smoothed or synthetically filled, statutory CPCB NAQI math is implemented strictly per official piecewise breakpoints, and all code and corrections are public.

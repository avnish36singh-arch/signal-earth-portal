CONTINUOUS REMOTE SENSING · INDO-GANGETIC AIRSHED

Independent Earth observation, ambient telemetry & atmospheric intelligence.

Signal Earth is an open-access environmental intelligence initiative synthesizing continuous ground telemetry (CPCB/UPPCB) with satellite earth observations (NASA MERRA-2, MODIS, Sentinel-5P) to evaluate particulate dynamics, boundary layer trapping, and climate microclimates with reproducible open-source science.

Observational Architecture

Scientific Pillars & Intelligence Framework

Signal Earth integrates multispectral satellite reanalysis with physical ground telemetry to build an auditable, multi-tier atmospheric surveillance framework.

Continuous Ground Telemetry

Real-time ingestion of continuous ambient air-quality telemetry across central industrial and transit corridors, applying rolling gap-fixed imputations and regulatory NAQI standard sub-indices.

CAAQMS · DPCC · UPPCB · CPCB

Satellite Remote Sensing & GIS

Coupling orbital thermal infrared reanalysis (NASA MERRA-2) with QGIS spatial microclimate zoning, measuring Land Surface Temperature (LST) and radiant skin decoupling dynamics.

NASA POWER · MODIS · QGIS GIS

Boundary Layer Inversion Physics

Investigating diurnal planetary boundary layer (PBL) collapse, nocturnal thermal stagnation ceilings, and riverine basin moisture traps that trigger 5-fold seasonal particulate multipliers.

Physical Meteorology · Inversion Modeling

Open-Access Reproducible Science

All analytics, data pipelines, and GIS models are published under permissive open-source licenses on GitHub with complete data provenance, null-fill notes, and public citations.

Python · Pandas · Plotly · Open Science

CIVIL & ENVIRONMENTAL ENGINEERING · HBTU KANPUR

Avnish Singh

Founder & Principal Investigator, Signal Earth

Conceived and developed by Avnish Singh during academic engineering studies at Harcourt Butler Technical University (HBTU) Kanpur. Signal Earth applies civil and environmental engineering frameworks to open telemetry streams — combining automated Python ingestion pipelines, regulatory CPCB sub-index mathematics, and QGIS vector microclimate mapping to provide rigorous, independent analysis of environmental phenomena across North India.

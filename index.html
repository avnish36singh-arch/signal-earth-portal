Spatial Particulate Dynamics & Seasonal Multipliers in Kanpur

CROSS-CITY COMPARATIVE SYNTHESIS DELHI NCR vs. KANPUR BASIN

Airshed Dynamics: Upper vs. Central Indo-Gangetic Basin

Direct side-by-side evaluation of 3,450 combined monitoring records across Delhi (Alipur) and Kanpur (Kalyanpur/Nehru Nagar). Comparing seasonal multipliers (5.75× vs. 3.56×), fine-particulate ratios (0.67 vs 0.57), and the contrast between regional agricultural smoke advection and riverine nocturnal inversion entrapment.

DISPATCH · MARCH 2024

CPCB National Clean Air Programme (NCAP): Regional Airshed Modeling Requirements

Independent assessment by atmospheric research consortiums evaluates NCAP target attainment across 131 non-attainment cities. Analysis demonstrates that municipal boundaries cover less than 15% of the total airshed area responsible for ground-level particulate mass in the Indo-Gangetic basin during winter inversions.

Source: Central Pollution Control Board / NCAP Review Portal (2024)

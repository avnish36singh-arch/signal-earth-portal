Independent Initiative

About Signal Earth

Signal Earth is an independent environmental research initiative exploring how our planet changes through satellite observations, open environmental datasets, and reproducible scientific analysis. We investigate atmospheric, hydrological, and land surface processes with zero sponsored bias.

Read about the initiative & governance →
Research Scope

Areas of Focus

01

Air Quality

Particulate pollution dynamics, continuous CAAQMS telemetry, meteorological boundary layer interactions, and seasonal aerosol multipliers.

02

Land Surface Temperature

High-resolution satellite radiometry, urban heat island zoning, impervious surface warming, and riparian cooling buffer dynamics.

03

Earth Observation & Open Data

Public sensor archives from NASA, ESA, USGS, and CPCB paired with documented, fully reproducible Python processing workflows.

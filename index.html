Earth Observation · Latitudinal Transect

Observing Planetary Changes by Latitude

Traverse satellite remote sensing observations across global latitudes: from polar cryosphere melt zones to regional aerosol basins and intra-urban microclimates.

01 / 05
LAT 78° 15' N · HIGH ARCTIC ZONE

Arctic Sea Ice Fracturing & Melt Margins

High-latitude satellite remote sensing documenting sea ice pack fragmentation, seasonal melt pond formation, and accelerated open ocean exposure along polar margins.

Instrument: Polar Orbit Earth Observation · Cryospheric Telemetry
LAT 28° 38' N · INDO-GANGETIC AIRSHED

Regional Agricultural Smoke Plume

Post-harvest biomass burning smoke spreading eastward along the Himalayan foothills, trapped under seasonal anticyclonic stagnation across northern India.

Instrument: NASA Terra MODIS · Corrected Reflectance (Nov 5, 2021)
LAT 26° 28' N · CENTRAL GANGA BASIN

Winter Boundary Layer Stagnation & Haze

Thermal nocturnal radiation inversion capping Kanpur and the central plains, compressing local particulate emissions into a dense, shallow surface layer.

Instrument: NASA Aqua MODIS · Winter Inversion Analysis (Nov 11, 2021)
LAT 26° 26' N · INTRA-URBAN MICROCLIMATES

30m Landsat 8/9 Thermal Infrared Radiometry

Cloud-native spaceborne thermal radiometry resolving a +3.60 K winter urban heat island in the dense core, uncovering microclimates masked by coarse reanalysis.

Instrument: USGS Landsat Collection 2 Level 2 · TIRS-2 Band 10
LAT 00° 00' · GLOBAL ORBITAL HORIZON

Global Atmospheric Boundary & Biosphere

Orbital perspective documenting the illuminated atmospheric limb, illustrating the razor-thin boundary layer that sustains the planetary biosphere against deep space.

Instrument: Satellite Earth Observation · Global Limb Photography

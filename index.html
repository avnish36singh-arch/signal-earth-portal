Kanpur Air Quality · Empirical Finding

Kanpur’s winter air carries 2.4 to 5 times the monsoon particulate load.

PM2.5, two monitoring windows (2021–22, 2025–26); range depends on how winter is defined.

Continuous PM2.5 Timeseries · NSI Kalyanpur & Nehru Nagar 855-Day Unmonitored Hiatus Marked
Reading the chart: Daily PM2.5 records from two regulatory CAAQMS monitors across 1,628 station-days. Data exist across July 2021 to October 2022 and February 2025 to August 2026. The 855 days between windows contain no public station records; no synthetic interpolation was applied.
2.4×–5.1×
Winter Particulate Multiplier

Winter PM2.5 compared to monsoon baseline. Standard winter (Dec–Feb) vs monsoon (Jun–Sep) averaged 2.40× across pooled data. Peak haze (Nov–Jan) vs core monsoon (Jul–Sep) reached 5.14× in 2021–22 and 3.01× in 2025–26.

+3.60 K
Landsat 30m Core Anomaly

Core runs +3.60 K above the rural ring in clear-sky mid-morning winter passes based on two scenes (Jan 15 and Feb 16, 2022), unmasking surface microclimates that coarse reanalysis cannot resolve.

Kanpur Winter Particulate Multipliers

Winter PM2.5 in Kanpur was between 2.4 and 5.1 times the monsoon level across two monitoring windows, with 48.9% of winter station-days exceeding statutory standards.

Analysis 02 · Regulatory Standards

Delhi NAQI Criteria Pollutant Dominance

Particulate matter (PM2.5 or PM10) governed the official daily AQI on 95.3% of valid monitoring days, while gaseous pollutants virtually never dictated the headline score in winter.

Period: 2017 to 2023 (1,137 official days, 731-day gap)
Source: CPCB / DPCC Site 204 (Delhi Alipur)
Single northern peripheral monitor along the NH-44 highway freight corridor.

Analysis 03 · Atmospheric Reanalysis

Kanpur Skin Temperature Association

Surface skin temperature exhibits a moderate negative correlation with PM2.5 (r = -0.455 across 308 concurrent pairs), reflecting shared regional seasonal cycles rather than direct causation.

Period: Jul 2021 to Oct 2022 (n = 308 pairs; 2,556 reanalysis rows)
Source: NASA POWER (MERRA-2) and UPPCB
Reanalysis cell is coarse (~55 × 60 km); correlation does not establish causation.

04 · Project Genesis

Signal Earth: From the Start

An independent initiative founded by a Civil Engineering student at HBTU Kanpur to examine environmental physics through transparent, reproducible data.

Origin & Focus

Civil Engineering Perspective

Signal Earth was started to examine how regional atmospheric boundary layer physics, civil transportation corridors, and urban morphology interact across northern India.

Rather than generic environmental summaries, the project conducts quantitative investigations directly on raw public archives from the Central Pollution Control Board (CPCB), UPPCB, DPCC, NASA, and USGS.

Reproducibility Rule

Zero Synthetic Imputation

Real-world regulatory stations fail, disconnect, or experience multi-month reporting hiatuses. Signal Earth enforces an uncompromising data rule: every operational gap is explicitly documented and never masked with artificial interpolation.

The 855-day hiatus in Kanpur and 731-day hiatus in Delhi are presented openly as unmonitored periods in every chart and table.

Epistemic Standard

Evidence Over Speculation

Numbers published on this platform must match an open Python script run logged in the public ledger. Interpretations must separate statistical correlation from physical causation.

Spatial resolutions are rigorously distinguished: 30-meter localized satellite radiometry is never conflated with coarse 55-kilometer global atmospheric reanalysis cells.

How this is made

All calculations run on open code against public regulatory archives and satellite records. Every number published on this site is traceable to an empirical script run and logged in an open ledger.

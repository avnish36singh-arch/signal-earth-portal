How Signal Earth Uses These Datasets

Global assessments provide necessary boundary conditions, but coarse resolution often masks local microclimates. Signal Earth bridges global reanalysis and ground truth by pairing institutional reports with 30m Landsat thermal radiometry, high-resolution MODIS plumes, and continuous station audits.

For example, while IQAir and CPCB document headline particulate numbers for northern India, Signal Earth's Kanpur investigation explicitly proves that an 855-day unmonitored regulatory hiatus occurred - enforcing a strict zero-synthetic-infilling policy.
